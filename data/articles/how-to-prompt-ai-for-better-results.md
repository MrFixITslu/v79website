# How to Prompt AI for Better Results

AI tools such as ChatGPT can be incredibly useful, but many people still use them in the simplest possible way.

They type something like:

```
Write me a business plan.
```

Then they get a generic answer and decide that AI is not very useful.

Often, the problem is not the AI. It is the instruction.

Think about a restaurant. If you walk in and say, **"Give me food,"** you will probably get something, but it may not be what you wanted. If you instead say, **"I want a chicken meal, nothing too spicy, under $30, and I need it quickly,"** the person helping you has much better instructions.

AI works in a similar way.

The clearer you are about what you want, the better your chances of getting a useful answer.

## The Simple Formula for Better AI Prompts

A strong prompt usually contains five things:

**Role + Goal + Context + Requirements + Output**

You do not always need every part, but this is a reliable structure when the task matters.

### 1. Role

Tell the AI what type of person or expert you want it to act like.

```
Act as a digital marketing specialist.
```

### 2. Goal

Tell the AI what you actually want to accomplish.

```
Help me create a marketing plan for my new cleaning business.
```

### 3. Context

Give the AI the background information it needs.

```
My business is based in Saint Lucia and mainly serves homes and small offices.
```

### 4. Requirements

Tell the AI about limits, rules or preferences.

```
Keep the marketing budget below $500 EC per month.
```

### 5. Output

Tell the AI what the finished answer should look like.

```
Give me a 30-day plan in a simple table.
```

## Weak Prompt vs Better Prompt

A weak prompt might be:

```
Write me a business plan.
```

A stronger version could be:

```
Act as a small-business adviser.

Help me create a simple business plan for a mobile car-washing business in Saint Lucia.

My customers will mainly be busy professionals and small businesses.

Keep the startup cost below $10,000 EC.

Include:
- services
- pricing ideas
- startup equipment
- estimated monthly expenses
- marketing ideas
- major risks

Write it in simple language that someone starting their first business can understand.
```

The second prompt works better because the AI has a role, a clear objective, useful context, limits and a specific output.

# 10 AI Prompts You Can Copy and Use

Replace anything inside square brackets with your own information.

## 1. Learn Something New

```
Act as a patient teacher.

Explain [TOPIC] to me as if I am completely new to it.

Use simple language and everyday examples.
Avoid unnecessary jargon.

After explaining it, give me:
1. The five most important things to remember
2. One real-world example
3. Five questions I can use to test my understanding
```

## 2. Improve Your Writing

```
Improve the text below without changing what I am trying to say.

Make it clear, natural and professional.
Use simple language.
Avoid overly formal words and phrases people would not normally use in conversation.

Here is the text:

[PASTE YOUR TEXT]
```

## 3. Brainstorm a Business Idea

```
Act as a small-business adviser.

I am considering starting this business:
[BUSINESS IDEA]

My location is:
[LOCATION]

My approximate budget is:
[BUDGET]

Analyse the idea and tell me:
- who the likely customers are
- what problem the business solves
- possible ways to make money
- startup requirements
- major risks
- competitors I should investigate
- three cheap ways I could test the idea before investing heavily

Use simple, practical language.
```

## 4. Create a Social-Media Post

```
Act as a social-media marketer.

Write a Facebook post promoting:
[PRODUCT OR SERVICE]

The target customer is:
[TARGET CUSTOMER]

The main benefit is:
[BENEFIT]

Make the post friendly and natural.
Do not make it sound like an aggressive advertisement.
Include a clear call to action.

Give me three versions with different opening lines.
```

## 5. Solve a Problem

```
Help me solve this problem:

[DESCRIBE THE PROBLEM]

Before recommending a solution:
1. Identify the most likely causes
2. Separate facts from assumptions
3. Give me the safest troubleshooting steps first
4. Explain what each step is testing
5. Tell me when I should stop and ask an expert

Use simple instructions.
Start with the most likely cause.
```

## 6. Make a Better Decision

```
Help me decide between these two options:

OPTION A:
[DETAILS]

OPTION B:
[DETAILS]

What matters most to me:
[YOUR PRIORITIES]

My budget or limitations:
[CONSTRAINTS]

Compare both options based on:
- cost
- benefits
- disadvantages
- risks
- long-term value

Recommend one option at the end and explain why.
```

## 7. Research a Topic

```
Help me research:

[TOPIC]

I want to understand:
[QUESTIONS]

Start with a simple explanation.

Then separate the information into:
- what is well established
- what may still be uncertain
- important facts I should verify
- questions I should investigate next

Do not present guesses as facts.
```

## 8. Create a Step-by-Step Plan

```
I want to achieve this goal:

[GOAL]

My starting point is:
[CURRENT SITUATION]

My deadline is:
[DATE]

My limitations are:
[TIME, MONEY, EXPERIENCE OR OTHER LIMITATIONS]

Create a realistic step-by-step plan.
Break large tasks into smaller actions.
Tell me what I should do first.

Also identify the three biggest things that could prevent me from reaching the goal.
```

## 9. Ask AI to Review Its Own Answer

```
Review the answer you just gave me.

Act as a critical reviewer trying to find weaknesses in it.

Identify:
- anything unclear
- unsupported assumptions
- missing information
- possible mistakes
- unnecessary complexity
- ways the answer could be more useful

Then give me an improved version.
```

## 10. Make AI Writing Sound More Human

```
Rewrite the text below so it sounds natural and human.

Keep the original meaning, facts and important details.

Use:
- natural sentence lengths
- simple everyday language
- contractions where appropriate
- a conversational rhythm
- occasional short sentences for emphasis

Avoid:
- unnecessary buzzwords
- repetitive sentence structures
- exaggerated claims
- generic introductions
- overly formal language
- phrases such as "in today's fast-paced world"

Do not intentionally add spelling or grammar mistakes.

Text:

[PASTE YOUR TEXT]
```

The goal of this prompt is not to trick people or to "beat" AI detectors. It is simply to make the writing clearer, more natural and more appropriate for the person reading it.

## The Biggest Prompting Secret: Keep Talking to the AI

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is treating AI like a search engine.

They ask one question, get one answer and stop.

You do not have to do that.

Try follow-ups such as:

```
That is too technical. Explain it more simply.
```

```
Give me a real-world example.
```

```
Turn this into a checklist I can follow.
```

Good prompting is usually a conversation rather than a single command.

## Tell AI What You Do Not Want

Sometimes better prompting means setting boundaries.

```
Explain this without using technical jargon.
```

```
Keep the answer under 500 words.
```

```
Do not give me ten options. Recommend the best three.
```

```
Do not assume I already understand accounting.
```

## Ask AI to Ask You Questions

If you are not sure what information to include, ask the AI to help you build the prompt.

```
I want you to help me create a business plan.

Before writing anything, ask me the questions you need answered to create a useful plan.
```

That turns the process into a guided conversation.

## AI Can Be Wrong

AI can sound confident and still be incorrect.

Be especially careful with medical information, legal advice, financial decisions, investments, important statistics, current news, government rules, contracts and technical instructions that could damage equipment or data.

For important information, ask:

```
Which parts of this answer should I independently verify?
```

```
What assumptions are you making in this answer?
```

```
What information would change your recommendation?
```

Confidence is not proof. Use AI to support your thinking, then verify important claims.

## Better Prompts Do Not Need to Be Longer

A good prompt does not have to be a page long. It just needs the right information.

Instead of:

```
Help me market my business.
```

Try:

```
Act as a marketing adviser.

Help me attract more local customers to my small bakery in Saint Lucia.

My marketing budget is $300 EC per month.

Give me the five most practical strategies I can start this month.

Rank them from highest to lowest priority.
```

## Final Thought

You do not need to master complicated AI terminology to use AI effectively.

Tell the AI:

- who it should act like
- what you want to accomplish
- what it needs to know
- what rules or limitations it should follow
- what you want the final answer to look like

Then keep the conversation going. Ask questions. Challenge the answer. Request examples. Ask for simpler explanations. Ask it to identify weaknesses.

**The better your instructions become, the more useful AI becomes.**

AI is most valuable when it helps you think, learn and work better — not when it replaces your own judgement.
